2. Contributor Ownership Summary & Scope

🟡 Yellow HH Role Permissions & Technicians

  • Role Permissions (SCRUM-12): Controls which sidebar tabs are shown/hidden per user role (Admin, Reception, Mechanic, Billing).
  • Route Gating: Blocks direct URL/navigation into restricted modules.
  • Mechanic Directory (SCRUM-22): Manages technician availability states and specialties.

🟠 Orange HH Customers, Job Cards & Staff

  • Admin User Creation (SCRUM-10): Admin creating and modifying staff accounts in Settings.
  • Customer Intake (SCRUM-16): Customer profiles, Sinhala Unicode support, duplicate phone warnings.
  • Job Cards (SCRUM-18, 19): Creating job cards, linking appointments, assigning mechanics.
  • Payment Recording (SCRUM-32): Recording partial cash/card installments.

🔵 WW (Blue) Auth, Appointments & FIFO Math

  • PBKDF2 Login (SCRUM-11): Salted cryptographic password verification and session state.
  • Appointments (SCRUM-17): Time slot scheduling and strict double-booking collision prevention.
  • FIFO Parts Deduction (SCRUM-26): Atomic deduction from oldest inventory batches upon job issue.
  • Auto-Billing (SCRUM-31): Automated calculation of labor charges, parts costs, and total invoices.

🟤 BH (Brown) Inventory Catalog, Prints & KPIs

  • Inventory Catalog (SCRUM-24): Stock items master, SKU codes, units, and categories.
  • Stock Danger List (SCRUM-28): Low stock threshold alerts and replenishment views.
  • Dual Printing (SCRUM-33): Standard A4 invoices, 80mm POS thermal receipts, reminder stickers.
  • Reports & KPIs (SCRUM-23): Daily financial summary, parts consumption, technician productivity.
